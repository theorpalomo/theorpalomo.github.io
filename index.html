Theo Palomo

Ph.D. Candidate in Economics

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the Paris School of Economics (PSE), supervised by Gabriel Zucman. I am affiliated with the Stone Center at PSE and the International Tax Observatory.

My research focuses on public finance, with particular emphasis on tax policy, optimal taxation, and wealth and income inequality in developing economies.

I hold an M.Sc. in Economics (APE) from PSE and a B.Sc. in Economics from the University of São Paulo (FEA-USP).

In Fall 2026, I am visiting UC Berkeley, hosted by Emmanuel Saez.
